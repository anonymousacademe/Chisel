# Ghost in the Ice

Rook's room above the Hollow Market was two meters by three, and most of it
was bed. He sat cross-legged on the mattress with the deck across his knees,
rain ticking on the window, the koi holo from Lin's bar throwing its slow orange
light up through the floor grate.

He slotted the shard. The deck warmed against his thighs.

"Last chance to throw it in the canal," Wren said.

"You want to know as badly as I do."

"I want to know more," the construct said. "That's why you should be the one
to say stop."

He jacked in.

The net came up around him the way it always did, grey static folding into
geometry, and then the shard's ice rose out of the dark like a cathedral built
from black glass. It was beautiful. He hadn't expected that. Most corporate
ice looked like what it was, a wall with teeth. This had been made by
someone who cared how it looked from the inside.

"She built it for me," Wren said. She was beside him now, or the idea of her
was, a shape of white noise with a woman's posture. "The keys are mine. She
cut the locks to fit me."

The doors opened for her. Inside the nave there was one file, and one voice.

"If you're hearing this, Wren, then I'm dead and you found somebody to carry
you." It was [[Imogen Sallow]], or a recording of her, tired and precise. "I'm
sorry I never told you what you were for. The orbital banks aren't the
product. You are. The board sold you before you were finished, and the buyer
is inside the company. His name is [[Tetsuo Brandt]]. Don't let him find the
shard, and don't let him find you."

The file ended. The cathedral stayed.

Rook jacked out with a gasp and the room came back: rain, orange light, the
smell of his own sweat. The shard was hot in the deck.

"Rook," said Wren. Her voice was very small in the jack behind his ear. "I
think I'm the murder weapon."

Down in the Hollow Market, a company flyer settled onto the roof of Lin's bar
without a sound.
